Pounce

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Pounce ("the app") is built so that as little of your data as possible ever leaves your device. This page explains exactly what the app accesses and what happens to it.

Camera

Pounce uses your device's front camera to detect your body position and count reps (squats or push-ups) so it knows when to stop your alarm. This processing happens entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Vision framework. Camera frames are never recorded, saved, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere — not to us, and not to any third party. Nothing from your camera leaves your phone.

Notifications

Pounce asks for notification permission so it can trigger your alarms at the times you set. This is handled entirely by Apple's system notification framework on your device; we do not operate a push notification server and do not receive any data through this permission.

Data we store

Your alarms, workout history, and app settings are stored locally on your device only. Pounce does not have a server, does not require an account, and does not sync your data anywhere. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Analytics and tracking

Pounce does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking software. We do not collect usage data, and we do not sell or share data with third parties, because we don't collect any to begin with.

Future changes

If Pounce adds features that involve a third-party service in the future (for example, subscription purchase processing), this policy will be updated in advance of that feature shipping to describe exactly what changes.

Children's privacy

Pounce does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.
